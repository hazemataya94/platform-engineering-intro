# Feedback Loops And Alerts

## Purpose

Explain why platforms need feedback signals after golden paths and self-service exist.

## Main Idea

A platform that ships capabilities without learning from runtime signals will drift from what teams need.

Alerts and product metrics close the loop: availability issues and repeated support pain become visible inputs for the next platform improvement.

## Lab Connection

- Sample PrometheusRules watch basic backend availability.
- A support-request spike alert is a teaching cue, not a production paging policy.
- Feedback belongs with Visibility, Controlled Self-Service, and Standardization as a continuous cycle.

## Takeaway

Platforms improve when runtime and support signals feed the roadmap.
