# Observability Self-Service

## Purpose

Explain why metrics and logs should be available without filing a ticket for every investigation.

## Main Idea

Visibility is not finished when a monitoring stack exists.

Developers still need safe, repeatable ways to explore metrics, logs, and product signals themselves.

Repeated support questions about resources, logs, or deployments are roadmap input for the platform team.

## Lab Connection

- Loki and Promtail collect cluster logs.
- Grafana dashboards turn raw signals into a shared investigation surface.
- The support-request exporter makes “ask another team” patterns visible as metrics.

## Takeaway

Self-service observability reduces interrupt-driven ops work without removing ownership of the application.
